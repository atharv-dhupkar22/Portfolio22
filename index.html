PhishRL: An End-to-End Reinforcement Learning Pipeline for Automated Email Threat Triage
2026 6th International Conference on Intelligent Technologies (CONIT) July 2026
PhishRL is an AI-powered email threat detection system that leverages Reinforcement Learning to automatically identify, classify, and prioritize phishing emails based on their risk level. The platform analyzes email content, headers, URLs, and attachments to generate intelligent threat scores, helping security teams respond more efficiently. By automating email threat triage, PhishRL reduces manual analysis time, improves detection accuracy, and strengthens organizational cybersecurity.
